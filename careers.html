Careers · Onsite in Multan

eCommerce Specialist

You will own live marketplace accounts for international brands — the listings, the stock, the orders and the customer messages — with written procedures behind you and a senior specialist to escalate to.

Apply for this role → Read the detail

Type

Full time

Location

Onsite — Gulgasht Colony, Multan

Shift

9 hours, including 1-hour break

Experience

2–3 years, required

The work

What you will do

  • Create and optimise product listings so they are found, and keep the catalogue free of errors and suppressions.
  • Monitor inventory, pricing and stock cover, and flag replenishment before it becomes a stockout.
  • Process orders, upload tracking, and keep late shipment rate inside target.
  • Answer buyer messages inside the platform's response window, and handle returns, refunds and claims.
  • Track account performance and prepare the weekly report for your accounts.
  • Work with the rest of the pod to hit project deadlines and keep the SOPs current as platforms change.

You

What we need from you

  • Two to three years of hands-on experience on Amazon, eBay or Walmart. This one is not negotiable — we need someone who has already worked inside a seller account.
  • Confident with product listing work and order management, including bulk uploads.
  • Clear written English — you will be writing to buyers and to clients.
  • Good time management. The queue does not clear itself, and the weekly report has a fixed date.
  • Available to work full time from our Multan office. This role is onsite; it is not remote or hybrid.

Us

What you get back

Systems

Documented SOPs for every recurring task, so you are not guessing at how something should be done.

Range

Real exposure to international brands across several marketplaces — not one account on repeat.

Progression

A route into senior specialist and account management roles as the client base grows.

Pay

Compensation set against your experience, discussed openly at offer stage.

Hours

Defined shifts in a stable office team. A growth-focused environment, not a churn one.

Support

A senior specialist to escalate to and a named backup, so nothing sits on one person.
