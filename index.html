How we work

Four stages, in this order.

The sequence matters. We do not touch bids before the catalogue is clean, and we do not write procedures before we know what is actually broken.

  1. 01

    Audit

    We go through the account as it stands: health metrics, catalogue errors, suppressed and stranded inventory, ad spend against margin, and where money is leaking. You get the findings whether or not you hire us.

    Weeks 1–2
  2. 02

    Systems

    Procedures written for your catalogue — not generic templates. Access and permissions set properly, escalation thresholds agreed, reporting cadence fixed, and the account owner and backup named.

    Weeks 3–4
  3. 03

    Run

    Daily operation by an assigned pod from our Multan office. Queues worked each morning, changes logged, and a weekly report that says what we did rather than only what happened.

    Month 2 onward
  4. 04

    Scale

    Once the base account is stable, we expand: new marketplaces, new countries, wider catalogue, or moving more of the range from FBM into FBA where the maths supports it.

    Quarter 2+

The operation

How the work is actually organised.

Agency results usually come down to whether the boring parts are documented. Ours are — and you see the same view of your account that we do.

Where we work

One office in Gulgasht Colony, Multan. The whole team is onsite — no distributed contractor network.

Written procedures

Every recurring task has a documented SOP, so cover during leave or illness does not change the output.

Shift coverage

Nine-hour shifts with an hour's break, scheduled to overlap UK and US East Coast business hours.

Account ownership

A named owner and a named backup on every account. You always know who to ask and who covers them.

Reporting rhythm

A weekly written report and a monthly review call. Numbers arrive on the same day each week.

Access & security

Role-scoped user permissions inside your own seller account. We never ask for your primary credentials.
